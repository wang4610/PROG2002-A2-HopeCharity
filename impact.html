How Your Donations Are Used

Every dollar raised through our events goes directly to frontline community support programs. We keep our overhead costs low through volunteer support, so over 92% of every donation reaches the people who need it most.

Funds are used for emergency shelter, food bank supplies, winter relief kits, youth mental health outreach, and after-school education grants for disadvantaged children.

Our Programs

Shelter Support: We provide temporary accommodation and rental assistance for families at risk of homelessness.

Food Bank Network: We stock local food pantries with fresh produce, pantry staples and baby supplies.

Youth Mental Health: We fund free counselling and outreach programs for at-risk teenagers.

Education Grants: We provide tutoring and school supply grants for children from low-income families.
